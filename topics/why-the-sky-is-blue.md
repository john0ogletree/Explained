# Why the Sky is Blue

Sunlight hits the atmosphere and **scatters**. Blue light scatters more
than red because of its shorter wavelength.

## Rayleigh Scattering

This is called *Rayleigh scattering*. The formula is:

```javascript
const intensity = 1 / Math.pow(wavelength, 4);
```

So shorter wavelengths (blue) scatter much more than longer ones (red).

- Short waves scatter more
- That's why the sky looks blue
- At sunset, light travels further and reds dominate

> Fun fact: on Mars, the sky is red at noon and blue at sunset.
